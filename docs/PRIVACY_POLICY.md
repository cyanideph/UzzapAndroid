# Uzzap Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 23, 2026

Uzzap is a social messaging application operated by CY. This policy applies to the Uzzap Android application (com.cy.uzzap).

## Information we process

Uzzap may process account and authentication information such as email address, user ID, username, nickname, profile information and account status.

Uzzap also processes content you voluntarily submit, including room messages, private messages, buddy activity, reports and other user-generated content.

Uzzap may process push-notification identifiers required to deliver notifications.

## How we use information

We use information to provide authentication, profiles, rooms, messaging, buddies, delivery/read receipts, notifications, blocking, reporting, moderation, UzzapBot features, security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, support and legal compliance.

## Service providers

Uzzap uses Supabase for authentication, database storage and realtime/backend services.

Uzzap uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications. Firebase Analytics collection is disabled in the application.

When UzzapBot's AI conversational feature is enabled and a message is selected for AI processing, relevant message text may be sent to Groq's API to generate a response. Passwords and authentication credentials are not intentionally sent to the AI provider as part of this feature.

## Chat content

Messages sent to a room may be visible to other members of that room. Private messages are intended for their participants, subject to security, reporting and moderation functionality.

Do not send passwords, authentication credentials, financial information, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information through chat.

## Moderation and reports

Uzzap processes information needed for blocking, reporting and moderation. Certain moderation or security records may be retained when reasonably necessary for abuse prevention, fraud prevention, security or legal compliance.

## Sharing

Uzzap does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers acting on our behalf, including Supabase, Firebase Cloud Messaging and, when AI functionality is used, Groq. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or others.

## Security

Uzzap uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards and encrypted network connections for supported backend communication. No Internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to operate Uzzap, provide messaging and moderation, prevent abuse and fraud, maintain security, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations.

Uzzap provides account deletion inside the app and an external web-based deletion request resource. When an account is deleted, associated account data is deleted unless specific information must be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention or legal reasons. Any retained information is limited to what is reasonably necessary.

## Children's privacy

Uzzap is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Users must meet applicable minimum-age requirements. Accounts created in violation of applicable age requirements may be restricted or deleted.

## Notifications and permissions

Uzzap may send notifications for messages, buddy activity, security events and other app activity. The current Android application uses Internet, network-state, notification and vibration permissions.

## Changes

We may update this policy when Uzzap's features, services, legal requirements or data practices change. The last-updated date will be changed when material updates are made.

## Privacy contact

Privacy questions and account-deletion requests can be submitted through the official Uzzap account-deletion web resource.
